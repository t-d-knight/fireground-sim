# Design rules

Standing rules for the exercise and the sim. Keep these when changing anything.

1. **Command tree.** Rule of 5: 5 crew per tanker, 5 tankers plus a command vehicle per strike team, 5 strike teams per sector. Orders go one level down only.
2. **Every number is sourced or tagged.** Anything not from a source goes in the Assumptions tab marked `A`, with who set it. No silent constants.
3. **Placeholders.** Mayday response procedure and red-flag wording are `[TK to insert]` until the brigade supplies them.
4. **Tone.** Direct and operational, no fluff. Dry humour is fine.
5. **Win condition.** The township is not impacted. A fire that is stalled but not contained is still a success if the residential area is protected and crews can be replenished for a second attack. The facilitator scorecard measures this.
6. **Defend is asset protection**, the last stand. It is not fire-edge work. Defenders hold at crew-protection reserve and draw directly from hydrants when they can.
7. **Realism over convenience.** Sectors, escalations above Make Tankers 20, and aircraft take time. The sim should make late calls hurt.
